# Clean-Room Handoff Audit

The auditor must ignore prior conversation and answer from repository documents only.

1. What is the product and its primary action?
2. What is in Launch MVP and explicitly out?
3. Which document owns each mutable domain?
4. What is the first authorized Work Package?
5. Which files may it create/modify and which are prohibited?
6. What public interfaces must it implement?
7. What exact commands and tests must run?
8. What Studio/manual evidence is required?
9. What performance/security limits apply?
10. How is the change rolled back?
11. What documents must be updated at Done?
12. Is any new human/product decision required before starting?

Pass only when question 12 is “No” and all other answers are concrete and internally consistent.
